3 Core Theory, Master Formulas & Exam Traps

1. Arc Length (Section 6.4)

Derived from $\Delta L = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2}$.

$$L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\,dx = \int_c^d \sqrt{1 + [g'(y)]^2}\,dy$$ $$L_{\text{param}} = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} \sqrt{\left(\frac{dx}{dt}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{dy}{dt}\right)^2}\,dt$$

2. Surface Area of Revolution (Section 6.5)

Sum of frustum bands $S = 2\pi r^*\,ds$ where $r^*$ is the radius to the axis of rotation.

$$\text{About } x\text{-axis: } S = \int_a^b 2\pi f(x)\sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\,dx$$ $$\text{About } y\text{-axis: } S = \int_a^b 2\pi x\sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\,dx = \int_c^d 2\pi g(y)\sqrt{1 + [g'(y)]^2}\,dy$$

3. Work & Hooke's Law (Section 6.6)

Force required to hold a spring stretched $x$ units beyond natural length is $F(x) = kx$.

$$W = \int_a^b F(x)\,dx = \int_a^b kx\,dx = \frac{1}{2}k(b^2 - a^2)$$

4. Centroids of Planar Regions (Section 6.8)

For a uniform lamina bounded by $y_{\text{top}} = f(x)$ and $y_{\text{bot}} = g(x)$ on $[a,b]$:

$$A = \int_a^b [f(x)-g(x)]\,dx, \qquad \bar{x} = \frac{1}{A}\int_a^b x[f(x)-g(x)]\,dx$$ $$\bar{y} = \frac{1}{A}\int_a^b \frac{1}{2}\left([f(x)]^2 - [g(x)]^2\right)dx$$

High-Yield ISRT Exam Traps

Trap 1 — Vertical Tangents Create Improper Arc-Length Integrals: In 2nd Incourse 2022 Q5 ($y = (4-x^{2/3})^{3/2}$ on $[0,2]$), $y' = -x^{-1/3}\sqrt{4-x^{2/3}}$ blows up at $x=0$. You must state that $\sqrt{1+(y')^2}$ is unbounded at $x=0$ and evaluate it as $\lim_{c\to 0^+}\int_c^2 2x^{-1/3}\,dx$.
Trap 2 — Wrong Radius in Surface Area: Always check which axis you revolve around! Revolving $y=x^2$ about the $y$-axis uses radius $r = x$ (not $y = x^2$), giving $S = \int_1^2 2\pi x\sqrt{1+4x^2}\,dx$, which integrates cleanly by $u = 1+4x^2$.
Trap 3 — Squaring Difference vs. Difference of Squares in $\bar{y}$: The moment $M_x$ uses $\frac{1}{2}\left([f(x)]^2 - [g(x)]^2\right)$, never $\frac{1}{2}[f(x)-g(x)]^2$.
Trap 4 — Natural Length vs. Total Length in Hooke's Law: $x$ in $F(x)=kx$ is always the displacement beyond natural length, not the total length of the spring.
